# Tamil Language

Tamil is a Dravidian language spoken predominantly in Tamil Nadu (India) and Sri Lanka. It is one of the longest-surviving classical languages in the world, with a literary tradition spanning more than 2,000 years.

Tamil has approximately 80 million native speakers worldwide. It is an official language of India, Sri Lanka, and Singapore.

In 2004, Tamil was declared a Classical Language of India, the first language to receive this distinction. The classical period of Tamil literature, known as Sangam literature, dates to approximately the 3rd century BCE to the 3rd century CE.

The Tamil script is distinctive. It uses 12 vowels (uyir ezhuthu), 18 consonants (mey ezhuthu), and 216 compound letters (uyirmey ezhuthu), totaling 247 characters. The script has changed very little since its classical period.

Common Tamil phrases: Vanakkam (வணக்கம்) means hello or greetings. Nandri (நன்றி) means thank you. Kaalai vanakkam (காலை வணக்கம்) means good morning. Eppadi irukkeenga (எப்படி இருக்கீங்க?) means how are you.

Tamil cinema, known as Kollywood and centered in Chennai (formerly Madras), is the second-largest film industry in India by number of films produced annually.

Tamil has one of the richest literary traditions of any Indian language. The Thirukkural, written by the poet Thiruvalluvar, is a classic Tamil text on ethics, politics, and love, still widely quoted today.
