# Hindi Language

Hindi is an Indo-Aryan language spoken primarily in northern and central India. It is written in the Devanagari script and is one of the two official languages of the Government of India, along with English.

Hindi has approximately 600 million native and second-language speakers, making it the third most spoken language in the world after English and Mandarin Chinese.

Hindi was declared an official language of India by the Constitution in 1950, which came into effect on January 26, 1950. September 14th is celebrated as Hindi Diwas (Hindi Day) to commemorate the adoption of Hindi as an official language of India.

The Devanagari script used for Hindi is also used to write Sanskrit, Marathi, Nepali, and several other Indian languages. It is an abugida, meaning each consonant carries an inherent vowel that can be modified or removed using diacritics. The script is written from left to right.

Standard Hindi draws heavily from Sanskrit vocabulary, while colloquial Hindustani (which overlaps with Urdu) incorporates significant Persian and Arabic loanwords.

Common Hindi phrases: Namaste (नमस्ते) means hello or greetings. Dhanyavaad (धन्यवाद) means thank you. Shubh prabhat (शुभ प्रभात) means good morning. Aap kaise hain (आप कैसे हैं?) means how are you. Mera naam (मेरा नाम) means my name is.

Hindi is the primary language used in Bollywood, the Hindi-language film industry based in Mumbai, which is one of the largest film industries in the world by number of films produced.
